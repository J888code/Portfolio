## Free Resources & Competitions Reference

### Competitive Programming & Interview Prep
- **Codeforces** (codeforces.com) - Weekly contests, free, very competitive
  - Use now (Y9+) for algorithmic thinking
- **AtCoder** (atcoder.jp) - Japanese platform, excellent quality contests
- **Google Code Jam** (codingcompetitions.withgoogle.com) - Free, prestigious, international (target Y12)
- **USACO** (usaco.guide) - Similar to BIO, great practice
- **LeetCode** (leetcode.com) - Free tier available
  - Start light in Y12 (easy problems)
  - Ramp up in Y13 for internship interview prep
  - Not needed right now (Codeforces is better)

### Pentesting / Cybersecurity Learning
- **Network Chuck's 15-hour Hacking Course** (YouTube) - Practical introduction you're already doing (7/15 hours complete)
- **Heath Adam'syt 15 hour course**
- **OverTheWire Bandit** (overthewire.org/wargames/bandit) - Linux fundamentals, completely free
- **TryHackMe** (tryhackme.com) - Free tier is solid, gamified hacking challenges
- **HackTheBox** (hackthebox.eu) - Free tier, harder challenges

### AI/ML Learning
- **fast.ai** (fast.ai) - Completely free, practical, excellent
- **Google ML Crash Course** (developers.google.com/machine-learning) - Free
- **Kaggle** (kaggle.com) - Free competitions, real datasets, notebooks

### Portfolio/Full-Stack Development
- **FreeCodeCamp** (freecodecamp.org) - Free, comprehensive
- **Frontend Mentor** (frontendmentor.io) - Free design-to-code challenges
- **GitHub** (github.com) - Free hosting, version control, open source

### General CS Fundamentals
- **MIT OpenCourseWare** (ocw.mit.edu) - Actual MIT courses, free
- **Khan Academy** (khanacademy.org) - Free fundamentals
